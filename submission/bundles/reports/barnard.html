Uncertainty

Controls

Limitations

  • Fixed circular aperture, not PSF fitting; a change in PSF width between visits leaks into the ratio for small apertures (see the robustness grid).
  • Only pairs at matching wavelength are compared, so most possible pairs are refused by design.
  • Fluxes are in pipeline image units; the ratio is the claim, not an absolute calibration.
  • Coordinates centre the cutout only; a fast-moving star is followed by re-centroiding.
