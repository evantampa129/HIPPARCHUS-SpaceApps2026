ASSETS, SOURCE, LICENCE
-----------------------
SPHEREx data (inside the Manim scenes and the app recording)
  remotion/public/img/rw_aur_a.png, rw_aur_b.png, rw_aur_diff.png, wise0855_a.png, barnard_epoch.png
  Copied from docs/img/. Real SPHEREx observations from the NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive (IRSA),
  SPHEREx DR1 / QR3, processed by HIPPARCHUS (cutouts, difference image; flagged pixels drawn in dark red).
  Data credit: SPHEREx / NASA/IPAC IRSA. Shown as processed observations, only scaled.

App screen recording (all three cuts)
  remotion/public/rec/app.mp4, marks.json. Recorded 2026-11-15 (UTC) from the real HIPPARCHUS app run
  locally (backend/ + frontend/dist), driven by remotion/scripts/record_app.mjs in headless Chromium
  (DevTools screencast). Cut into short segments at 1x speed, gently zoomed. During the recording IRSA answered
  HTTP 502, so the app used its cached archive listing; the app labels this ("CACHED listing") and the
  video says so. The analysis itself ran from the project's cache of real SPHEREx cutouts.
  Project's own work (see ../LICENSE).

Manim scenes (most of every cut)
  remotion/public/manim/m01–m07, m09–m15 *.mp4, copied from visualizations/manim/renders/. Rendered by the project's
  Manim scene library; every number on screen is read from submission/evidence/*.json and checked by
  visualizations/manim/tests/. Each clip carries its own label (DATA-DRIVEN, EXPERIMENTAL, CONCEPTUAL, SIMULATED) and
  source line. M07 (shift-and-stack for a distant planet) is SIMULATED; M15 is CONCEPTUAL. Project's own work.

On-screen claims
  Every number comes from the project's results: RW Aur x2.56 (pair A+B, unresolved), 175σ formal / 12.4σ with the
  12.5% calibration floor, 24/24 robustness, Barnard's Star 0/58 usable exposures (refused), Kleopatra 31 detections
  in 7 visits vs JPL Horizons at 0.15–0.18″ rms. No cut claims a detection of Planet X: HIPPARCHUS has not found it.

Audio
  remotion/public/audio/ambient*.wav (one per cut, transitions on that cut's scene changes), synthesised by remotion/scripts/make_ambient.sh with ffmpeg
  (sine partials + filtered pink noise). No music, no samples, no third-party audio.
  Loudness: two-pass ffmpeg loudnorm to -16 LUFS integrated, true peak <= -1.5 dBTP.
  NO VOICE: no narration recording and no synthetic voice (no local TTS engine was installed).
  The narration text is shown as burned-in English captions and in the .srt.

Software

  team AETHENOVA is a single person, so no paid licence is required.
  ffmpeg / ffprobe, Chromium headless shell, playwright-core (recording and page crops only).
