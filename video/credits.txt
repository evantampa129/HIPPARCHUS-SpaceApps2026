Programmatic graphics (made in code, remotion/src/scenes.tsx)
  Scene 1 celestial sphere, star field and ecliptic: ILLUSTRATION (labelled on screen).
  Scene 2 RA/Dec chart and reticle: ILLUSTRATION (labelled); the coordinates of RW Aur are real.
  Scene 6 moving dot over five epochs: CONCEPT — FUTURE RESEARCH, ILLUSTRATION (labelled). Not data.
  It does not show, and the video does not claim, any detection: HIPPARCHUS has not found Planet X.
  Labels, dates and numbers are taken from the project's results (RW Aur x2.56, 175σ formal / ≈12σ
  with the calibration floor, 24/24 robustness, Barnard's Star 58/58 flagged, refused).

Audio
  remotion/public/audio/ambient.wav, synthesised by remotion/scripts/make_ambient.sh with ffmpeg
  (sine partials + filtered pink noise). No music, no samples, no third-party audio.
  Loudness: two-pass ffmpeg loudnorm to -16 LUFS integrated, true peak <= -1.5 dBTP.
  NO VOICE: no narration recording and no synthetic voice (no local TTS engine was installed).
  The narration text is shown as burned-in English captions and in the .srt.
